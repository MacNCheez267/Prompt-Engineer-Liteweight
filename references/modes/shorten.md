# Shorten mode

Remove repeated ideas, filler, and unnecessary wording. Retain every essential task, condition, named item, number, constraint, and requested output. Do not make the prompt so terse that a relationship or requirement becomes unclear. If a detail appears redundant but could change the meaning, keep it or ask.

## Example

**Before**

> Please write a concise summary that briefly explains the main ideas from the article and avoid going into too much detail, while keeping the important points.

**After**

> Summarize the article concisely, covering its main ideas and essential points.

The revision removes overlapping brevity instructions and keeps the requested coverage.
