# Polish mode

Make the smallest changes needed to correct grammar, spelling, punctuation, and awkward wording. Keep sentence order and phrasing where possible. Do not strengthen, weaken, or reinterpret instructions. If a phrase is ambiguous, preserve it or ask rather than silently choosing a meaning. Do not restructure unless a minor change is needed for grammatical clarity.

## Example

**Before**

> Write a email to my team about meeting moved to Friday and ask them confirm.

**After**

> Write an email to my team explaining that the meeting has moved to Friday and asking them to confirm.

The revision corrects grammar and wording while preserving the request.
