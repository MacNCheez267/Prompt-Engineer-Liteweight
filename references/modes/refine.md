# Refine mode

Improve clarity, specificity, and usefulness while remaining faithful to the user's stated intent. Make implicit relationships explicit only when the prompt supports that reading. Do not invent context, audience, criteria, or deliverable details. If a material detail is missing, ask before revising; if it is not material, leave it open rather than guessing.

## Example

**Before**

> I need you to help me understand renewable energy and give me examples.

**After**

> Explain renewable energy and include examples.

The revision removes conversational filler while retaining both requested elements.
