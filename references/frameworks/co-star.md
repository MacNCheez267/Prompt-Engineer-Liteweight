# CO-STAR framework

## Components

- **Context:** The situation or background that informs the response.
- **Objective:** The goal the response should achieve.
- **Style:** The manner of presentation, such as plain-language or technical.
- **Tone:** The intended emotional register, such as formal or warm.
- **Audience:** The people the response is for.
- **Response:** The requested content and form of the answer.

## When to use

Use CO-STAR when the audience, style, or tone strongly shapes the result, especially for writing, teaching, and communication prompts.

## How to adapt

Do not fill in style, tone, or audience from assumption. Include those fields only when the user supplied them or after clarification. They may remain optional if they are not essential. Keep the objective and response distinct when doing so improves clarity; merge overlapping fields rather than repeating the same instruction.
